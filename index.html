Research

Computational medicine

I did my M.Eng. at Kumoh National Institute of Technology in South Korea (2023 to 2025), in the CML lab, writing CUDA code to simulate heart cells. These are the topics I work on now.

Cardiotoxicity, in silico

Simulating human ventricular cell models (ORd and ToR-ORd) on GPUs to predict how a drug changes heart rhythm, following the CiPA approach.

ECG classification

Fusing statistical and physiological features of ECG signals before classification, and testing whether the fusion actually helps.

Brain CT segmentation

Rotation-equivariant networks that find lesions in brain CT scans.

Infant movement

Classifying infant movement across datasets, with collaborators at PENS.

Working on a paper or a data project of your own? I also help other teams with code, analysis and manuscripts. See the AI and data page.
